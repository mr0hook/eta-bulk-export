Privacy Policy — ETA Bulk Export

Last updated: 11 July 2026 · Data controller: Omar Soliman

ETA Bulk Export handles data in two independent flows: (1) your invoice & receipt content, which stays on your device and is never sent to us; and (2) the registration & account data you provide to unlock the tool, which is sent to the developer's backend. Both are described below. The always-current version of this policy is served at https://eta-bulk-leads.omsoliman95.workers.dev/privacy.

1. Your invoice & receipt data — stays on your device not sent to us

When you use it on https://invoicing.eta.gov.eg, the extension:

  • reads the access token the ETA portal already stored in your browser (localStorage.USER_DATA),
  • uses it to call the ETA API (https://api-portal.invoicing.eta.gov.eg) to download your own e-invoices / e-receipts (the same data the portal shows you), and
  • writes the resulting Excel/JSON files to a folder you pick.

This content is attached only to requests to api-portal.invoicing.eta.gov.eg (enforced by an origin check in code) and written only to your chosen local folder. It is never transmitted to the developer, to the backend below, or to any third party.

2. Registration & account data — sent to the backend

Bulk export requires a free, one-time registration. The following is sent to the developer's backend at eta-bulk-leads.omsoliman95.workers.dev (hosted on Cloudflare):

You explicitly consent via the checkbox on the registration form before anything is sent. This data is not sold and is processed only by the operator and the named providers (Cloudflare; Disify).

What is stored on your device

  • localStorage — export run state (so you can resume); your registration credentials (device id, a per-device key, the last licence verdict).
  • IndexedDB — a handle to the output folder you granted, so it can be reused.
  • The exported files — in the folder you chose.

Retention

Account records are kept until you request deletion or the operator removes the account. Admin action logs auto-expire after 180 days; anti-abuse counters expire within hours.

Permissions and why

  • invoicing.eta.gov.eg and api-portal.invoicing.eta.gov.eg — run on the portal and read your invoice/receipt data from the ETA API.
  • eta-bulk-leads.omsoliman95.workers.dev — the registration / licence backend (section 2).
  • The output-folder picker uses the File System Access API (explicit selection each time; no separate permission).

Your choices & rights

Registration is required only for bulk export. You can stop using the extension and request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing the developer at omsoliman95@gmail.com. Uninstalling stops all further data collection.
