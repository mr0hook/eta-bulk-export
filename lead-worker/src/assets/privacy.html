ETA Bulk Export — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026 · Data controller: Omar Soliman

ETA Bulk Export is a Chrome extension that lets an Egyptian taxpayer bulk-export their own e-invoices and e-receipts from the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) portal to Excel/JSON files. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles and where it goes. There are two independent data flows.

1. Your invoice & receipt data — stays on your device not sent to us

Your invoices, receipts and their line items are read on your device from the ETA API (api-portal.invoicing.eta.gov.eg), using the access token the ETA portal already stored in your browser for your own session. They are written only to a folder you pick on your computer. This content is never transmitted to the developer, to the backend server described below, or to any third party.

2. Registration & account data — sent to the backend

Access to bulk export requires a free, one-time registration. Registration and the ongoing licence checks send the following to the developer's backend at eta-bulk-leads.omsoliman95.workers.dev (hosted on Cloudflare):

How your data is used

Registration data is used to provide managed access to the tool, to enforce the access controls above, and to contact you about the tool. It is not sold. It is processed only by the operator and the service providers named here (Cloudflare for hosting and storage; Disify for email validation).

Retention

Account records are kept until you request deletion or the account is removed by the operator. Administrative action logs auto-expire after 180 days. Server error logs auto-expire after 30 days. Anti-abuse counters expire automatically within hours.

Export records: the part-by-part breakdown of a date range and the progress messages are deleted as soon as that export completes successfully. They are kept while an export is unfinished, because they are exactly what lets you resume it — deleting them early would lose your place. The summary row (date, range, document type, totals) is kept with your account.

Your choices & rights

Registration is required only for bulk export; you can stop using the extension and request deletion of your account at any time. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, email the developer at omsoliman95@gmail.com. Uninstalling the extension stops all further data collection.
